# Restore integrated temporal-clause punctuation

In chapter 27, change “when, you’ve got a name” to “when you’ve got a name.” The cited 1915 scan (p. 338) supports the uninterrupted temporal clause; [PG37106](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37106) retains the comma. A rhetorical pause is conceivable in isolation, but the retained source comparison and local syntax favor this high-confidence restoration. This does not identify the original transcription event.
