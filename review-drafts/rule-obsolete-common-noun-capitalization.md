# obsolete common noun capitalization

Lowercase Algebra in chapter 7, Freshmen in chapter 12, and both instances of Opera in chapter 41. These denote a school subject, a college class, and an unnamed musical work respectively; none functions as a proper title. The supplied Manual capitalization rule requires removing obsolete common-noun capitals when meaning is preserved. These four corrections have high confidence and do not assert a transcription error.
