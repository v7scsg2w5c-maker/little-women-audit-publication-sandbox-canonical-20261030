# restrained restrained disambiguation

Change restrained to re-strained in Meg’s jelly-making passage in chapter 28. The preceding picking, boiling and straining, followed by “reboiled, resugared, and restrained”, makes repeated straining the relevant sense. The hyphen clarifies that sense without changing the action. Recommend this as a discretionary editorial clarification with medium confidence; the original remains interpretable, and no lost hyphen or authorial intention is established.
