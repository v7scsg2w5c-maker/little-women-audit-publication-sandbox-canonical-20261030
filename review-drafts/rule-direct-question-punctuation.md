# direct question punctuation

In chapter 24, change the end of Meg’s question from “the servants laugh at her,” said Meg to “the servants laugh at her?” said Meg. “Why didn’t you” introduces a direct question, answered by her mother’s “I did.” The question mark makes that syntax clearer while preserving the lowercase speech attribution. This is a medium-confidence, discretionary Editorial revision, not a demonstrated transcription restoration.
