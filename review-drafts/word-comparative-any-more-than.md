# Restore comparative any more than

In chapter 23, restore “anymore than” to “any more than.” The cited 1915 scan (p. 288) has the separate words in Meg’s comparison, “My John wouldn’t marry for money, any more than I would.” [PG37106](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37106) retains the joined form. Select the printed alternative because its wording fits this comparative construction; do not claim the joined form was historically impossible. This is a high-confidence local restoration.
