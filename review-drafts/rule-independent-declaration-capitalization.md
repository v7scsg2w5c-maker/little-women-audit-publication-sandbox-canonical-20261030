# independent declaration capitalization

Capitalize the starts of five independent declarations within dialogue: “There ain’t” after “Beth?” (chapter 17), “The burn” after “Whatever shall I do?” (chapter 3), “Half of” after “I hope not!” (chapter 30), “She’s so ambitious” after “Dear little girl!” (chapter 36), and “She won’t think so” after “Bless her dear heart!” (chapter 44). Each clause states something new rather than attributing the preceding speech. These are medium-confidence, discretionary Editorial capitalizations under modern-style guidance; inherited rhetorical lowercase is not evidence of transmission loss.
