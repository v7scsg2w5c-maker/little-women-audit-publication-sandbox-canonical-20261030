# Restore as soon as

In chapter 38, restore “as so on as” to “as soon as.” The retained [PG37106](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37106) passage and the cited 1915 scan (p. 494) support soon, matching the temporal connective. This is a high-confidence restoration relative to those sources; their agreement does not locate the first introduction of the damaged spelling.
