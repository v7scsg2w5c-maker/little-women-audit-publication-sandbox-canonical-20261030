# Pocket handkerchief compound consistency

Change the two singular “pocket-handkerchief” occurrences in chapters 24 and 25 and the plural “pocket-handkerchiefs” in chapter 3 to open forms. They name the same object and align with the existing open form in chapter 24. Step 17 recommends consistency; choosing the open spelling remains an editorial judgment. Medium confidence; the census covers this lexical family, not every compound.
