# Jardin public lexical choice

In chapter 37’s Nice narration, change “Jardin Publique” to “Jardin Public.” Public agrees with the masculine jardin, and retained naming reports support that form for the garden in this context. Recommend this as a medium-confidence, discretionary Editorial choice under the owner’s better-reading preference. Preserving the transmitted irregularity remains defensible; the proposal does not prove Alcott’s intention or a transcription error. The place-name stays in plain roman text under the owner’s names-not-language policy.
