# Compound seedcake

Change “seed-cakes” to “seedcakes” in chapter 33, where Minnie feeds the boys. The retained dictionary observation supports the edible cake sense and its ordinary plural. This is a medium-confidence, optional Editorial modernization; it preserves the food, number and meaning rather than correcting a demonstrated source error.
