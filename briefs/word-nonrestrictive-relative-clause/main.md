# Restore the nonrestrictive relative-clause comma

In chapter 13, insert the comma in “exclaimed Meg, who felt grown up already, having just reached seventeen.” The cited 1915 scan (p. 181) supplies the delimiter, while PG37106 omits it. The added clause describes the already identified Meg. This is a high-confidence local restoration; in the composed markup the comma remains outside Meg’s name span.
