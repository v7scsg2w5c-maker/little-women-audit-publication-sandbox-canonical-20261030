# Restore cafés plural

In Amy’s Paris letter (chapter 31), restore café to cafés. The retained PG37106 passage has the plural, as does the cited 1915 scan (p. 397); the existing six-copy review reports the same ending across the applicable print copies. This is a high-confidence restoration of the final s, preserving current typography. The copies are not six independent authorial decisions, and the exact historical import is not established.
