# Speech introducer comma

In chapter 17, replace “said gravely;” with “said gravely,” before Jo’s assessment of Beth’s illness. The comma connects the reporting phrase directly to its ensuing speech; the existing semicolon provides a stronger but intelligible pause. Recommend this as a discretionary editorial modernization with medium confidence. §1.4.2 supplies the Editorial disclosure requirement, not a mandatory punctuation rule, and no print-restoration claim is made.
