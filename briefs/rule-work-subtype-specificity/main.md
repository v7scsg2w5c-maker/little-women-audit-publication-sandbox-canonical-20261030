# Work subtype specificity

Use the play subtype for Schiller’s Mary Stuart and Death of Wallenstein, and recommend book for The Sea-Lion. Retained genre evidence supports the plays, while the Sea-Lion choice follows the local account of Fred drawing material from a favourite book. These are discretionary refinements: book remains a broadly valid description for the plays, and a bound-periodical reading of The Sea-Lion remains possible. Confidence is high for the plays and medium for The Sea-Lion.
