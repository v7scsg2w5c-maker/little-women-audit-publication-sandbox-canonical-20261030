# Parenthetic and causal comma

Add four commas: bracket “in spite of all obstacles” in chapter 4 and “as they called it” in chapter 32; mark the clause boundary in “rich in that line, for, on New Year’s Day” in chapter 33; and close “after long travelling and much uphill work” in chapter 34. Each addition clarifies a particular clause or aside while retaining its words and existing pauses. These are discretionary editorial improvements, with high confidence for chapter 4 and medium confidence for the others. Alternative punctuation remains coherent; the chapter-32 reading has contrary transmission support. No lost punctuation or authorial intention is established, and §1.4.2 governs Editorial disclosure rather than requiring these commas.
