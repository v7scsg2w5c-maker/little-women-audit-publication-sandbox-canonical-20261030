# Restore the direct-address comma

In chapter 9, restore “I wouldn’t Meg;” to “I wouldn’t, Meg;”. The cited 1915 scan (p. 119) supplies the comma separating Meg as the person addressed. PG37106 omits it. Select this precise printed delimiter with high confidence, retaining the current contraction and semicolon; variant punctuation elsewhere in the witnesses is not part of the change.
