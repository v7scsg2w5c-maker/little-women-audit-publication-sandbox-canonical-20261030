# Restore outer quotation closure

Add the missing outer closing double quotation mark after “spider.” in Amy’s chapter 19 reply and after “Jo.” at the end of her sister’s January letter in chapter 33. The cited 1915 scan supports the marks on pp. 244 and 433. The first reply ends before the parrot speaks; the second closes a letter whose literal opening quotation marks remain throughout. These high-confidence restorations preserve the inner quotation, signature span and period. PG37106 shares Amy’s omission; other digital versions use a different letter presentation, which should not be imported here.
