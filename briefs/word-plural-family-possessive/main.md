# Restore the plural family-name possessive

In chapter 4, restore “At the Kings today” to “At the Kings’ today.” The cited 1915 scan (p. 52) has the apostrophe, and the paragraph names the King parents and children, supporting a reference to their household. PG37106 and an earlier Part First OCR reading omit it. The qualified printed reading and local possessive function favor this high-confidence restoration; retain today rather than importing printed to-day.
